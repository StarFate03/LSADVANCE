What We Make

Services

L&S Advance delivers four core fiberglass and FRP manufacturing services, each fabricated to specification.

Fiberglass Services

Design, molding, and finishing of fiberglass-reinforced components for industrial and commercial use — handled end-to-end, from specification to finished product.

FRP Process Tanks

Corrosion-resistant FRP vessels for storing and processing chemicals and industrial fluids — built to order for manufacturing and processing plants.

Fiberglass Fabrication

Custom fiberglass parts and structures, fabricated to your specifications and engineering drawings — made to order, not from a fixed catalog.

Subcontract Manufacturing

We produce fiberglass and FRP components on your behalf, as an extension of your production line — ideal for overflow capacity or when you don't fabricate in-house.
