About L&S Advance

L&S Advance Sdn. Bhd. is a Malaysian fiberglass and FRP manufacturer headquartered in Banting, Selangor, in continuous operation since December 26, 2014 — over 11 years serving the Malaysian FRP manufacturing sector.

L&S Advance is a family-run manufacturing business, built on direct hands-on fiberglass fabrication experience. The company operates from its facility in Banting, Selangor, producing FRP process tanks, custom fiberglass fabrication, and subcontract manufacturing work.

02

Services

L&S Advance delivers four core fiberglass and FRP manufacturing services, each fabricated to specification.

Fiberglass Services

Fiberglass services cover the design, molding, and finishing of fiberglass-reinforced components for industrial and commercial applications. L&S Advance handles this process end-to-end, from initial specification through to finished product.

FRP Process Tanks

An FRP process tank is a corrosion-resistant vessel built from Fiber Reinforced Plastic, used to store or process chemicals and other industrial fluids in manufacturing and processing plants. L&S Advance fabricates FRP process tanks to order.

Fiberglass Fabrication

Fiberglass fabrication is the custom manufacturing of fiberglass parts and structures built to a client's own specifications and engineering drawings. L&S Advance fabricates to order rather than from a fixed catalog.

Subcontract Manufacturing

Subcontract manufacturing means L&S Advance produces fiberglass and FRP components on behalf of other companies, working to their specifications as an extension of their own production line. It suits clients who need overflow capacity or don't run in-house fiberglass fabrication.

What is an FRP process tank used for?

An FRP process tank is used to store or process chemicals and industrial fluids. FRP is chosen for this purpose because it resists corrosion in ways that metal tanks often cannot.
