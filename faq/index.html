Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we're asked most often about our fiberglass and FRP manufacturing.

What does L&S Advance manufacture?

L&S Advance manufactures fiberglass and FRP (Fiber Reinforced Plastic) products, including FRP process tanks and custom fiberglass fabrication, for industrial and commercial clients in Malaysia.

Where is L&S Advance located?

L&S Advance is based in Banting, Selangor, Malaysia, at Lot 173, Jalan 3, Kompleks Perabot Olak Lempit, 42700 Banting.

What is an FRP process tank used for?

An FRP process tank is used to store or process chemicals and industrial fluids. FRP is chosen for this purpose because it resists corrosion in ways that metal tanks often cannot.

Does L&S Advance do subcontract manufacturing?

Yes. L&S Advance produces fiberglass and FRP components on behalf of other companies, working to their specifications as an extension of their existing production line.

How long has L&S Advance been in operation?

L&S Advance has been in operation since December 26, 2014 — over 11 years of continuous fiberglass and FRP manufacturing in Malaysia.

What is fiberglass fabrication?

Fiberglass fabrication is the custom manufacturing of fiberglass parts and structures, built to a client's own specifications and engineering drawings rather than mass-produced from a fixed catalog.

Does L&S Advance work from custom drawings or specifications?

Yes, L&S Advance fabricates to client-supplied specifications and drawings for custom fiberglass and FRP components.
