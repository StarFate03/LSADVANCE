About L&S Advance

A Malaysian fiberglass and FRP manufacturer in Banting, Selangor, in continuous operation since December 26, 2014.

L&S Advance Sdn. Bhd. is a Malaysian fiberglass and FRP manufacturer headquartered in Banting, Selangor, in continuous operation since December 26, 2014 — over 11 years serving the Malaysian FRP manufacturing sector.

L&S Advance is a fiberglass and FRP manufacturer built on direct, hands-on fiberglass fabrication experience. The company operates from its facility in Banting, Selangor, producing FRP process tanks, custom fiberglass fabrication, and subcontract manufacturing work.

As a subcontract manufacturing partner to FlexiDynamic Holdings Berhad, L&S Advance fabricates FRP components to specification for established players in the industry.

Every project is fabricated to specification rather than mass-produced from a fixed catalog, so clients receive fiberglass and FRP components built to their own requirements and engineering drawings.
